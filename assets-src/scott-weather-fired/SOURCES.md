## Denver arrival "corner posts" (item 4; added by the image agent)

Commons has no image of the DEN corner posts and no DEN STAR (arrival) chart. Its only DEN charts are airport diagrams.

What the corner posts are. DEN arrivals come in through four arrival gates on the diagonals, called the "corner posts". Each corner holds two arrival fixes. Departures leave through four gates between them, on the sides: north, south, east and west. The NWS paper's figure gives the classic fixes, with DEN at the centre of the cross:
- NW: RAMMS / TOMSN
- NE: LANDR / SAYGE
- SE: DANDD / QUAIL
- SW: POWDR / LARKS

The paper says: "Thunderstorms in a gate can cause a gate swap … If most or all the gates are shut off, little or no traffic can arrive or depart." It also notes that afternoon storms usually form over the mountains to the west first, which hits the two west posts and the west departure gate.

Current procedures: the FAA's October 2026 STAR list for KDEN (from airnav.com/airport/KDEN; the charts are at aeronav.faa.gov/d-tpp/2610/09077<NAME>.PDF) still includes the conventional LANDR, RAMMS, TOMSN, DANDD, QUAIL, POWDR and LARKS arrivals, alongside RNAV STARs (AALLE, CLASH, FLATI, LAWGR, LONGZ, NIIXX, SSKII, TBARR). I have not checked which RNAV STAR feeds which corner. Draw the four corners on the NE/SE/SW/NW diagonals and the departure gates N/S/E/W. The figure is schematic and gives no distances.

Orientation notes for the director's files. The USGS 2002 aerial is north-up and about 1 m/px in the 10800 px original, so about 2.8 m/px at 3840 px and about 10.8 km across the frame. I measured this from runway 7/25 (12,000 ft). The photo is from April 2002, so the westernmost runway, 16R/34L (16,000 ft, opened 2003), appears only as a dirt strip under construction. Trace the runways from the FAA diagram. In the FAA's 2024 PDF diagram (Commons File:09077AD.pdf) the printed page is rotated so that north points to the LEFT; rotate it 90° clockwise to get north-up. A north-up 3840 px render is in the image agent's scratch, in `ref_extras/`.
