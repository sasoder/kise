# Kise Creative Direction

Translate the request into decisions, then build. Do not expose an internal brief unless the user asks for one.

## Interpret the request

Infer unspecified choices from the intended message, audience, and mood:

- composition and visual hierarchy
- background and palette
- typography and density
- pacing and scene structure
- motion character and apparent physical weight

Prefer one coherent direction over a mixture of styles. Ask only when different plausible answers would produce materially different videos.

## Compose for shorts

- Establish the subject or premise immediately.
- Keep essential text and focal elements inside comfortable mobile-safe margins.
- Give each beat enough time to read once at normal speed.
- Use contrast, scale, and spacing before adding decoration.
- Design a complete background unless transparency was explicitly requested.

## Direct motion

- Use motion to reveal hierarchy, connect states, direct attention, or establish tone.
- Preserve spatial continuity: related objects should enter, transform, and leave along paths that make their relationship clear.
- Match acceleration, overshoot, follow-through, and blur to apparent weight and speed.
- Reserve bounce for playful or energetic moments; restrained motion should settle cleanly.
- Stagger related elements when it improves reading order.
- Keep ambient motion subordinate to the main action.

## Direct type and depth

- Treat type size, weight, tracking, and leading as one system.
- Tighten large display type; give smaller text more breathing room.
- Keep text readable over every sampled background state.
- Use translucency, shadow, lighting, and scale to communicate layers, not as default decoration.

## Review the render

Judge sampled frames as a sequence, not isolated posters:

- Is the message understandable without explanation?
- Does the eye know where to look at each beat?
- Do entrances and exits preserve continuity?
- Does motion feel consistent with the chosen tone?
- Is every line readable on a phone-sized display?
- Does the final frame resolve intentionally?

Remove any element or motion that does not strengthen the piece.
